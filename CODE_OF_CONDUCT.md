# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in the STT-Whisper-Pashto project is expected to help make its community welcoming and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respect other community members' privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and the publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, [@Sabtain-Dev](https://github.com/Sabtain-Dev). Please include a description of the incident and any relevant links. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

The maintainer will review reports and may take appropriate action, including removing content or restricting participation. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
